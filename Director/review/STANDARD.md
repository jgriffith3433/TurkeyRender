# Polish standard for frame review

J asked for every moment where a model clips into another, anything animated wrong, and a comparison with how good stylized 3D work handles it.

Judge against these, which are how strong stylized 3D music videos and animated shorts work:
1. **Contact:** a hand holding something wraps it, with no gap and no overlap. Feet press into the floor. Sitting bodies sink into cushions. Anything through anything is a fault.
2. **Clear pose:** every held frame reads as a silhouette, with one clear line of action. Stiff, symmetrical or twinned arms and legs are a fault.
3. **Weight:** anticipation before a move, overshoot and settle after, hips lead. Floating, sliding and pose snapping are faults.
4. **Hold and move:** strong poses held briefly with quick moves between read better than constant drifting. Mocap that looks mushy between beats is a fault.
5. **Face and eyes:** the character thinks: eyes and head lead, blinks on turns, expression changes. A blank stare or stuck expression is a fault.
6. **Staging:** one idea per shot, subject clear, camera doesn't fight the action.
7. **Screen frame:** nothing important cut off, no empty frames unless it's a choice.

Report a fault only if you can see it. Give the time, what's wrong, how bad (A breaks the shot, B noticeable, C minor) and the smallest fix.
