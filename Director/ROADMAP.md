Goal: a polished, fully real-time 3D animated music video that tells the story in `STORYBOARD.md` and holds up beside high-end music videos (`POLISH.md`). J directs. J does no work in Unity.

A milestone is done when it has been looked at and passes, not when the work is finished.

## After the 17:45 assembly: quality passes

The assembly proves the story. These make it really good. Each has its own agents, and each is reviewed by someone who didn't make the work.

1. **Story and acting.** Does every beat read without explanation? Are his reactions believable? Rework any moment that's unclear or flat.
2. **Animation.** Weight, anticipation, follow-through, secondary motion, hands and contact. No sliding, popping or clipping. This is the pass that separates animation from moving models.
3. **Sets and art.** Show sets get full dressing and real materials, so none look like primitives. Every frame has foreground, background and detail.
4. **Lighting and colour.** Every shot lit by hand against `COLOR_SCRIPT.md`. Composition checked as stills.
5. **Timing and edit.** Frame-exact hits, shot lengths, transitions, sound effects mixed under the song.
6. **Independent review.** A fresh reviewer scores it against `POLISH.md`. Anything below the bar goes back. Repeat until it passes.

## After v7: the animation pass (top priority, J 2026-11-05)

J: the story works, the animation must be much better.
Cause: 110 whole-body moves, 30 clips of 2,700, 26 pose cues in 204 seconds, no root motion, no overlap.

1. **Tools.** Root motion, pose-to-pose keys, automatic anticipation and settle, successive timing, overlap, locked contacts, motion review sheet.
2. **Shot list.** Every shot he or a turkey is in gets a list of key poses, chosen clips and contacts before it is animated.
3. **Blocking.** Strong silhouettes on the key beats, checked as stills.
4. **Splining.** Timing, arcs, weight. Checked in stepped motion, not stills.
5. **Polish.** Hands, faces, eyes, overlap, contact.
6. **Review.** Fresh reviewer, frame-stepped against POLISH.md. Below the bar goes back.
