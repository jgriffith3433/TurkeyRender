# Hawkes Punch: space, the epic version (draft 3, for J)

J, 2026-11-08: "make the planets actual planet size. This should be epic in space... think One Punch Man", "don't use jokes like Right in the… Mars. Try something a little better". This replaces scenes 5-8 and the button's space part in SCRIPT.md and SPACE-PLAN.md. The clinic, the punch, the ad and the button's clinic part stay. The craft rules come from `research/zero-g-acting.md` and `research/epic-anime-action.md` (in progress). This file is the story and the shots.

## The comedy
The bigger the feat, the smaller the reaction. Two ordinary guys do impossible things at planetary scale and talk about them as if it were nothing. No puns. The laughs come from:
- understatement after spectacle;
- absurd literal-mindedness ("People live there.");
- the deadpan beat after the boom.

## The scale rules
- **Planets are real size.** A man is a speck next to a moon, and a moon is a speck next to Jupiter.
- **Scale reads through the cut:**
  - a tight shot on a face;
  - then an extreme wide where he is a dot against a lit world, with speed lines and the event (a ring breaking, a moon cratering);
  - then back to the face for the deadpan.
- **Superhuman means superhuman:** he can hold a planet overhead like Atlas, or catch Jupiter and slide back across its orbit. The physics is the spectacle. The joke is that nobody acts impressed.
- **One hard sun from screen left.** Planets bounce coloured light onto them. Rim light always.

## The scenes (about 80-90 s)

### 5. ARRIVAL: silence (keep the beat)
- **EXTREME WIDE.** A streak of light leaves Earth's atmosphere and shoots past the Moon. Speed lines, silent. It's him, screaming.
- **WIDE.** He sails out past the Moon toward Saturn. Saturn grows from a dot to the whole frame. His scream cuts dead the instant he crosses into the ring plane. He coasts to a stop, a tiny figure against the rings. Hold.
- **MCU, lit by ring light.** HIM: "Silence." *(hold)* "...Nope. More music."
- **MCU, push in.** HIM: "Wait. Space... She's up here." He pulls out the drawing of the faceless sheet ghost.

### 6. THE SEARCH: suspense
- **WIDE.** He hops across the ring, from a house-sized ice boulder to the next, holding up the drawing.
- **MEDIUM.** He peers around a boulder. **Behind him, the ghost drifts up from behind another boulder and looks at him.** He turns; she's gone. Twice. *(We know; he doesn't.)*
- **MCU.** A tap on his shoulder. He freezes. HIM: "...Space ghost?"
- **TWO-SHOT.** He turns: it's HIM, in a red tee. The double looks at the drawing, then at him.
- DOUBLE *(flat)*: "That's a sheet."
- HIM: "She has a vibe."
- **CLOSE.** The double boops his nose and drifts backward, grinning, then is gone in a streak.

### 7. THE CHASE: escalation in three, then the break
*His goal: catch him and get an answer. Each stunt is bigger, and each ends on a deadpan beat.*

**7a. Through the rings (speed).**
- **CHASE CAM.** The double blasts through the ring. The ice explodes behind him in a long wake.
- **WIDE.** He follows through the same hole. The wake is twice as big.
- **MCU, both flying side by side, wind-blown.** HIM: "Are you her?" DOUBLE: "No." HIM: "Are you sure?"

**7b. The moon (power, by accident).**
- **WIDE.** The double hides behind a small grey moon.
- **MEDIUM, him, sneaking along the moon's surface.** **CLOSE:** the double pops up: "BOO!"
- **IMPACT.** His startle flings out a fist and hits the moon. **Impact frame, then a shockwave.** A crater the size of a city blooms on the moon.
- **WIDE, held.** The two of them float above a moon with a giant fresh crater. Dust settles.
- **TWO-SHOT, deadpan.** They both look at it. HIM: "...That was there before." DOUBLE: "Sure."

**7c. The worlds (spectacle).**
- **MEDIUM.** The double grabs the edge of Saturn's ring and pulls. **The whole ring comes off like a hoop.**
- **EXTREME WIDE.** He spins it overhead like a lasso and throws it. It loops over **Jupiter**.
- **WIDE.** He yanks. Jupiter starts moving.
- **ON HIM, low angle, Jupiter filling the sky behind and coming at him.** A long anticipation as he braces.
- **IMPACT.** He catches Jupiter: hitstop, an impact frame, a shockwave across Jupiter's clouds. He slides back thousands of km in one long wide, his heels carving speed lines in nothing.
- **MCU, him, palms flat on a wall of storm clouds, a hurricane bigger than Earth swirling beside his head.** Beat.
- HIM *(calm)*: "Okay."
- He shoves it back. It goes back to where it was.

**7d. The break: the rule of three breaks.**
- **WIDE.** The double winds up overhead, Atlas-style, with a blue-and-white planet. Clouds. City lights on the night side.
- **MCU, him.** HIM: "Hey."
- **MCU, the double, mid-wind-up.** DOUBLE: "What."
- **INSERT.** The Earth, close: the city lights.
- **MCU, him.** HIM: "People live there."
- **MCU, the double.** He looks at the Earth. He looks at the city lights. Long beat.
- **WIDE.** Very carefully, he flies it back and sets it into orbit like setting down a full glass. He gives it a small pat. It keeps turning.
- **TWO-SHOT.** HIM: "Did anyone see?" DOUBLE: "Everyone saw."

### 8. THE STOP (keep the heart)
- **WIDE.** They float side by side above Earth at sunrise, out of breath. Earth's terminator glows behind them.
- **MCU, him.** "Are you... the space ghost?"
- **MCU, the double.** "No, man. I'm you."
- **MCU, him, push in.** He looks at his face. "You do have a face." He looks at the drawing and lets it go. It drifts off toward the sunrise.
- **WIDE.** The drawing drifts past the ghost, who catches it, looks at it, and seems pleased. Neither man notices. He starts to turn; the double turns his chin back. DOUBLE: "Hey. Focus."
- **TWO-SHOT to lens.** DOUBLE: "Are you feeling like there's two of you?" Into the ad.

### Button (space part)
- **EXTREME WIDE.** A streak leaves Earth again, the next day. He coasts in above Earth.
- **TWO-SHOT.** The double is waiting with two cans. DOUBLE: "Same time tomorrow?" They clink and drink. Behind them, the ghost floats with the drawing and waves at us.

## What it takes (no shortcuts)
- **Real-scale space set:**
  - Saturn with walkable ring boulders;
  - a cratering moon;
  - Jupiter with a moving storm;
  - Earth with day/night and city lights;
  - the Moon;
  - the sun;
  - a starfield;
  - ring debris.
- **Effects:**
  - impact frames;
  - speed lines;
  - shockwave rings;
  - ring-ice explosion wakes;
  - the crater bloom;
  - hitstop that freezes the bodies too;
  - camera shake;
  - motion blur.
- **Hero animation keyed pose to pose for every stunt:**
  - anticipation, release and follow-through;
  - the Atlas hold, the lasso spin, the catch-and-slide, the brace, the boulder hops, the flying pose;
  - on the zero-g base, never ground clips.
- **Camera:** chase cams, low heroic angles, the face-to-extreme-wide scale cut, held deadpan two-shots.
- **Sound:** silent space with impacts felt as low rumbles, and the deadpan lines in dry, close voices.

- Space: 40 shots, 73.390-173.528 s (100.14 s).

- Average shot: 2.57 s.
- Plain register: 68% of screen time. Epic register: 32%. Kept on purpose: the deadpan is the comedy.

**Look column:**
- E = epic register: low or close wide lens, hard rim, shake and effects allowed.
- P = plain register: eye level, centred, 50-85 mm, locked off, near silence.
- E>P and P>E switch on a beat inside the shot. The two registers are never blended.

**Blocking:**
- HIM is always screen left and the DOUBLE screen right, and every camera stays on the -z side of their line.
- Dialogue two-shots face about 120°/240° (180 = lens). Singles look 15-25° off the lens toward the partner.
- Contact with a planet is shot at human scale on a terrain patch (near layer). The disc is shown only from far away (far layer).

**Pose plan:**
- Every body's base is `zg_neutral` (NBP).
- Hero beats are keyed pose sheets, named in the actors column:
  - F1-F3: flight
  - S1-S3: startle and strike
  - H1: hop
  - P1-P3: peer, sneak and deadpan float
  - B1: boop
  - D1-D2: drawing up, let-go
  - R1-R3: ring pull, lasso, yank
  - C1-C4: brace, catch, slide, shove
  - A1-A2: Atlas lift, set-down
- Acting steps on 3s for HIM (deadpan) and 2s for the DOUBLE, and goes to 1s only inside strikes. The camera always runs on 1s.

**24 fps:**
- `"fps": 24` in `style.json` reaches `mv shot`, `mv render` and `mv final` through `fpsOf`.
