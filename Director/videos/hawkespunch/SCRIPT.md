# HAWKES PUNCH: shooting script, draft 2

Written 2026-11-08 to the rules in `Director/research/story-and-script-craft.md`. Supersedes REWRITE.md. About 3:20. No music: voices, sound effects, room tone. Nothing is built until J approves.

**Logline.** A man who can't stop hearing music, and who is in love with a ghost in space, asks his doctor for help. She prescribes Hawkes Punch, every day until the symptoms go away. She means it literally.

## Setup / payoff ledger
| Planted | Where | Paid off | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| He hears music no one else can (drums his fingers, hums) | 1, 2 | "Silence." ... "Nope. More music." | 5 |
| Poster: "HAWKES PUNCH. Ask your doctor." | 1 | It's literal; the ad at the end | 3, 9 |
| Framed boxing gloves on her wall: "GOLDEN GLOVES CHAMPION" | 2, held insert | The punch (we knew it was coming) | 3, 4 |
| "She doesn't have a face. But she has, like... a vibe." | 2 | "You do have a face." The faceless ghost waves at us. | 8, 10 |
| "Every day until the symptoms go away." | 3 | The next day, punched again: "Same time tomorrow?" | 10 |
| The turkey receptionist | 1 | Looks up at the ceiling crash, then back to her screen | 4 |
| The red can | 3 | The ad; the can rolls at the very end | 9, 10 |

**Suspense, not surprise.** The audience sees the boxing gloves before he does (the bomb under the table). In space, the audience sees the ghost behind him while he searches the wrong way.

**Rule of three.** His three symptoms (music, a ghost, in space). Three looks for the ghost, the third a tap. Three chase gags that grow (BOO, kick, Mars), then the pattern breaks (the Earth).

## 1. INT. CLINIC WAITING ROOM: DAY (0:00-0:12)
*He wants help. Obstacle: waiting. Turn: called in (+).*

**WIDE (establishing).** A pale green clinic. A TURKEY in a nurse's cap at reception, typing. On the wall, a red poster: **HAWKES PUNCH. Ask your doctor.**

**MEDIUM.** HIM (30s, purple tee, a little green-faced) in a waiting chair. His fingers drum his knees to a beat only he hears. He hums under his breath.

**INSERT.** The poster. Hold 1.5 s.

TURKEY *(o.s.)*: Gobble.

**MEDIUM.** He looks up, stands, goes in. *(Cut on his movement.)*

## 2. INT. EXAM ROOM: DAY (0:12-0:58)
*He wants a cure. Obstacle: his symptoms are absurd. Turn: she knows exactly what he needs (+, for now).*

**WIDE (establishing).** The exam room. Bed, desk, window. The DOCTOR (40s, **white coat**) sits at the desk, kind smile. He sits on the bed. On the wall behind her: a frame with **red boxing gloves** and a plaque.

**MEDIUM CLOSE, her.** Warm, pen ready.

DOCTOR: So. Tell me about your symptoms.

**MEDIUM CLOSE, him, facing her (eyelines match).** He launches in, a little too honest.

HIM: Okay. So. I can't stop listening to music. Like, ever. In the shower. In my sleep.

**MEDIUM CLOSE, her.** Nods. Writes.

HIM *(o.s.)*: I tried silence once, and it was just... more music.

**MEDIUM CLOSE, him.** Lower, shy. *(Slow push-in from here: the realisation is his.)*

HIM: And, um. I'm in love. With a ghost.

**INSERT, her pen.** It stops.

HIM *(o.s.)*: In space.

**MEDIUM CLOSE, her.** She looks up at him. Blank. *(Hold: the pause before the punchline.)*

**CLOSE, him.** Dreamy.

HIM: She doesn't have a face. But she has, like... a vibe.

**MEDIUM CLOSE, her.** Deadpan. Writes it down.

DOCTOR: A space ghost.

**CLOSE, him.**

HIM: ...Space ghost.

**INSERT (held 2 s).** The boxing gloves on the wall. The plaque: **GOLDEN GLOVES CHAMPION**. *(We see it; he doesn't. The bomb under the table.)*

## 3. INT. EXAM ROOM: CONTINUOUS (0:58-1:13)
*He wants the cure. Obstacle: the cure is her fist. Turn: hope to doom (-).*

**MEDIUM, her.** She smiles, slides a red can across the desk.

**INSERT.** The can: **HAWKES PUNCH. One a day until it stops.**

DOCTOR: Hawkes Punch. Every day until the symptoms go away.

**MEDIUM, him.** He picks it up, relieved. Turns it.

HIM: So I just... drink it?

**MEDIUM CLOSE, her.** Pause.

DOCTOR: No.

**MEDIUM, her.** She stands. Takes off her glasses. Glances at the gloves.

**MEDIUM, him.** He follows her look to the gloves (eyeline). Back to her.

HIM *(small)*: Oh no.

**MEDIUM CLOSE, her, low angle.** She rolls her shoulders. Flat, calm, terrifying.

DOCTOR: Don't worry. It's only just a healthy amount of people in the world. You are just one of them. So shut up, and swallow the damn Hawkes Punch.

## 4. THE PUNCH (1:13-1:24)
*Payoff of the gloves and the poster.*

**LOW 3/4 FRONT, her.** The wind-up. Her face goes cold.
**CLOSE, him.** The hit. His head snaps. *(Whoosh-crack, the loudest sound in the film; a 3-frame freeze.)*
**WIDE.** He rockets up through the ceiling. Tiles rain.
**MEDIUM, reception.** The TURKEY looks up at the rattling ceiling. Looks back at her screen. Types. *(Free laugh.)*
**WIDE, sky.** He streaks up through clouds. The sky goes from blue to deep blue to stars. He screams.

## 5. EXT. SPACE, "THE PLAYGROUND": CONTINUOUS (1:24-1:40)
*He wants to understand where he is. Turn: he realises she is up here (+, a goal).*

**HARD CUT. WIDE (establishing, held 3 s).** The scream cuts dead. Total silence. He drifts to a stop. Behind him, filling the back of the frame, a huge lit **JUPITER** (screen right, all sequence). Around him float **small planets the size of beach balls**: a red one, a ringed one, a little blue-and-green one. *(The world rule: up here, planets are small enough to pick up.)*

**CLOSE (lit).** His face. Silence.

HIM: Silence.

*(Beat. Hold.)*

HIM: ...Nope. More music.

**CLOSE.** A thought lands. He brightens. *(Slow push-in: the realisation.)*

HIM: Wait. Space. ...She's up here.

## 6. SPACE: THE SEARCH (1:40-1:55)
*He wants the ghost. Obstacle: he looks the wrong way. Turn: he's found, but by the wrong one.*

**MEDIUM, him screen left.** He looks left, hopeful. **Behind him, screen right, a pale faceless sheet ghost drifts into view.** *(We see her; he doesn't.)*
He turns right. The ghost slips behind a little planet.
**MEDIUM.** He turns left again. The ghost peeks out on the right. *(Second time: the pattern.)*
**CLOSE, him.** A tap on his shoulder from behind. He freezes. Hope.

HIM: ...Space ghost?

**TWO-SHOT, side.** He turns. It's HIM, in a **red tee**, grinning. *(The double, introduced clearly: same face, different shirt. Third time, the pattern broken.)*

**CLOSE, the double.** He reaches out and boops his nose.

DOUBLE: Boop!

**CLOSE, him.** Confused. Then offended.

## 7. SPACE: THE CHASE (1:55-2:25)
*He wants answers. Obstacle: the double won't stop playing. Turn: rivals to truce.*

**WIDE.** The double kicks off and zips away left to right past the little planets, laughing.

DOUBLE: Ha!

**MEDIUM, him.** He pushes off after him.

HIM: Hey! Are you her?!

**CHASE, side (always left to right, Jupiter behind).** They swim-fly across frame.

*Gag 1, the scare.* **MEDIUM.** The double ducks behind the ringed planet. He arrives and looks around. Nothing. **CLOSE TWO-SHOT.** The double pops up behind him.

DOUBLE: BOO!

HIM *(high)*: Aah!

**CLOSE, him.** Embarrassed. The double laughs off-frame.

*Gag 2, the kick.* **WIDE, side.** He lunges; the double spins and boots him in the backside. He tumbles across frame.

DOUBLE: You're it!

*Gag 3, the throw.* **MEDIUM, him.** He stops tumbling. A little red planet floats by. He grabs it. Looks at it. Looks at the double. Grins.

HIM: Oh, it's on.

**WIDE, side (one shot, built for it).** He throws. The double catches it in the gut.

DOUBLE *(winded)*: Right in the... Mars.

*The break.* **CLOSE, the double.** A slow evil grin. He reaches out of frame and comes back winding up with the little **blue-and-green planet**.

**INSERT.** It's Earth. Tiny clouds turning.

**TWO-SHOT.** He freezes mid-dodge.

HIM: Dude. Dude. That's the Earth.

DOUBLE: I know. I know.

**TWO-SHOT (one 4 s shot).** They both gently place it back where it floated.

BOTH: Sorry, everybody.

DOUBLE: Nobody saw that.

## 8. SPACE: THE STOP (2:25-2:42)
*He wants the ghost. Obstacle: the truth. Turn: lonely to not alone (+).*

**TWO-SHOT, close, Jupiter behind.** Out of breath, they float face to face.

**SINGLE, him.** Shy.

HIM: Are you... the space ghost?

**SINGLE, the double.** Gentle.

DOUBLE: No, man. I'm you.

**SINGLE, him.** He looks at the double's face. *(Pause.)*

HIM: You do have a face.

**WIDE TWO-SHOT.** Side by side. Behind them the ghost drifts past and waves at us. He starts to turn toward it; the double turns his chin back.

DOUBLE: Hey. Focus.

**TWO-SHOT to lens.** Both turn to camera with TV smiles.

DOUBLE: Are you feeling like there's two of you?

## 9. INT. TV STUDIO: THE COMMERCIAL (2:42-3:05)
*The film becomes the ad it was always selling.*

**The fold.** Space folds away into a pastel daytime-TV studio. A giant can. The two of them on stools.

HIM *(to lens)*: Is it right for me?

DOUBLE: It's right for everyone like you.

HIM: That's me!

**MEDIUM, the clinic set.** The DOCTOR at her desk, not looking up. Lower third: **A REAL DOCTOR / Not looking up**.

DOCTOR: I've prescribed it to a healthy amount of people.

**TWO-SHOT, stools.** They drink.

HIM: Mmm! Tastes like a fist!

DOUBLE *(fist pump)*: A good fist.

**FULL-FRAME CARD (held so it can be read).** The side-effects crawl, read fast and deadpan by the ANNOUNCER, listing everything we've just seen, ending:

ANNOUNCER: ...if you are allergic to punches, your doctor will punch you.

**CARD (2 s).** **HAWKES PUNCH. Ask your doctor.**

## 10. INT. EXAM ROOM: THE NEXT DAY (3:05-3:20)
*Payoff of "every day".*

**WIDE.** The same room. A card: **THE NEXT DAY**. He sits on the bed. She writes, not looking up.

DOCTOR: Any symptoms?

**MEDIUM CLOSE, him.** Dreamy.

HIM: Well, I met someone, and he—

**MEDIUM CLOSE, him.** Her fist enters frame from off screen. *(Crack.)* **HARD CUT.**

**WIDE, the playground.** He rockets in and drifts to a stop. The DOUBLE floats there waiting, holding two cans.

DOUBLE: Same time tomorrow?

**TWO-SHOT.** He takes a can. Smiles. Behind them, out of focus, the ghost waves at us.

**BLACK.** A red can rolls into frame. Stops.

## Production notes (for the build, from SPACE-STAGING.md)
- Space is one place: Jupiter always screen right and lit. He's always screen left and the double screen right, until the swap on "You're it!". Planets are beach-ball sized and never change size on screen.
- Physical gags are wide and from the side, one action per shot. Dialogue is close and against Jupiter with face fill.
- Room tone everywhere, never digital silence. The only true silence is scene 5, and it's held long enough to read as a choice.
- New props: the boxing-gloves frame and plaque, the white coat, the "THE NEXT DAY" card, two cans for the double.
- New voice lines (cents): "So I just... drink it?", "No.", "Oh no.", "Wait. Space. ...She's up here.", "Hey! Are you her?!", "Oh, it's on.", "Hey. Focus.", "Same time tomorrow?". Everything else is already voiced.
- Cut from v1: the lasso, the satellite, the Saturn swing, the volley, the asteroid chase, the comet scare, and every planet-sized throw.
- The test before polish: a cold viewer watches the previs with no script and retells it. Every gap from this script gets a fix.
