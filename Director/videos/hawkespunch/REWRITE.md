# Hawkes Punch: the rewrite (draft for J)

J, 2026-11-08: "there's A LOT of work to be done on this entire script. Especially in the space it didn't really make sense at all." This is the new space sequence (and fixes elsewhere), one line per shot: what we see, and why it is there. Nothing is built until J approves it.

## Why v1's space did not work
- **No place.** Backgrounds flipped between blue and beige; planets changed size and position every cut; we never knew where anyone was.
- **No goal.** Stunts happened for no reason, with no cause, result or reaction on screen.
- **Scale broke.** A planet as big as a person, then a speck; people 2 pixels tall beside planets.
- **Five throw chains** that did not line up across cuts.

## The rules this version follows
1. **One place.** "The playground": a calm patch of space with huge, lit **Jupiter** filling the back of every shot (same side of frame all sequence), and a few **small planets floating at beach-ball size** around them. The world rule is shown once, early, and never broken: up here, planets are small enough to pick up. Earth is one of them.
2. **One goal.** He is looking for the space ghost he told the doctor about. Everything in space is him chasing that idea.
3. **Every gag is set up, pays off, and gets a reaction**, and is shot from the side or 3/4 against Jupiter, so we always see who did what to whom.
4. **He is screen left, the double screen right**, all sequence, except when they swap on purpose.
5. **One throw gag, built for it.** Everything else is chase, scare, kick and faces.

## What a cold viewer got from v1 (COLD-VIEW.md, watched with no script)
- **Worked:** the turkey receptionist, the literal punch launching him into orbit, and the ad whose side effects recap the film.
- **Space unreadable:** his size changes every shot, the background flips colour, and you can't tell who hits whom.
- **The double:** appears with no setup. "That's himself" only lands 90 s later.
- **Static talk:** too much talk over static close-ups (0:23-0:55 on a mechanical 2.5 s rhythm; 17 s holding the Earth).
- **Clinic eyelines never meet:** she stares at the desk and he stares at the ceiling. She jumps between standing and sitting across cuts.
- **The title gag goes by in half a second:** no visible can, and no big sound.
- **The ending has no payoff.**
- **Dead silences sound like dropouts.**
- **His look changes:** his arms are a different colour from his face, and his hair and lighting change mid-video.
- **The space-ghost thread never resolves.**

## Clinic (0 to 83 s): fixes
1. **The turkey stays and pays off.** Open on the turkey receptionist, the best opening image. At the punch, cut to her at reception as the ceiling crash shakes the room: she looks up, then back to her screen. It's free and it's funny.
2. **The doctor reads as a doctor.** White coat, a clear medium on her face early, and one warm smile, so the later cold turn shows.
3. **Eyelines meet.** He looks at her when he talks and she looks at him, matched across cuts. Her writing cutaways come after a look.
4. **She's either sitting or standing, never jumping between them.** She sits at the desk for the symptoms, stands up on "Hawkes Punch", and stays standing.
5. **The monologue breathes.** Cuts land on his pauses and her reactions, not on a 2.5 s clock. One cutaway shows her pen stop on "a ghost".
6. **The title gag is the punchline, and it reads.**
   - The can is in shot when she says "Hawkes Punch" (a close insert of the red can).
   - The punch is a clean 3-beat: wind-up on her face, the hit on him, the flight.
   - A big whoosh-crack sound above everything.
7. **The silences are deliberate.** Wherever the room goes quiet, we hear room tone or a faint ring, never digital zero. The only true silence is the space gag.

## Commercial and button
Keep the structure: studio, both on stools, the can, the doctor's testimonial ("A REAL DOCTOR / Not looking up"), the fist pumps, the fast side-effects read. Fixes:
- The text now renders. "ASK YOUR DOCTOR" holds for 2 s, and the side-effects crawl finishes its sentence.
- The clap is cut, and the stools section is tightened (no static 10 s).

**A new button that pays off "every day until the symptoms go away":**
1. The clinic, the next day. She doesn't look up: "Any symptoms?"
2. Him: "Well, I met someone, and he—"
3. The fist, from off screen. Hard cut.
4. Him flying back into the playground, Jupiter behind. His double is waiting, holding out two cans. "Same time tomorrow?"
5. Black. The can rolls in. The end.
