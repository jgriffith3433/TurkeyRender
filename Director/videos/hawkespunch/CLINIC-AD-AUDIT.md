# Clinic and ad audit: every shortcut and gap

Written 2026-11-08 against `quick-full-mix.mp4` (rendered 2026-11-07 23:27; 223.46 s, 854x480, 24 fps). It covers the clinic (0-73.4 s), the ad (173.5-202.8 s) and the next-day button (202.8-223.5 s). Space is ignored. The bar is the one J set: "make the entire video how we wanted it without taking all these shortcuts". He rated the animation 1/10.

**How it was watched.** Contact sheets at 2 fps over every part. 24 fps bursts on the punch (61.9-65.2), the flight (65.2-68.5), the can toss (38.2-41.5), the glove look (47.7-51.0), the next-day glove (208.9-210.6) and the drink (183.7-187.0). A 9 fps burst on the fist pump (186.4-190.8). Full-size stills at 1.5, 3.5, 8.6, 10.5, 16.5, 20.3, 32.5, 44, 65.6, 176, 181.5 and 206.5. 12 fps face crops of both speakers (13.0 and 53.0). A 0.25 s RMS and peak level timeline of the mix. I read the cards (`cards/*.yaml`), `cut.json`, `acts/70-lines.json`, `acts/75-captions.json`, `acts/80-sfx.json`, `acts/00-cast.json`, SCRIPT.md, COLD-VIEW-2.md, the research and the clip catalog.

**What I couldn't do.** I didn't hear the audio, so sound items come from levels plus the cue list. Lip sync is judged from the face crops. The render is a quick render: no AA, low resolution. Picture items are about content, staging and light, not sharpness.

Shot times below are from `cards.map.json`, and they match the cuts detected in the render.

## The ten that matter most (in order)

1. **The punch isn't a punch** (61.99-64.79). She throws a hook at the air about 1.5 m from him. There's no anticipation hold and no contact. The hit is a washed-out, half-transparent frame from behind him, and the mix clips at 0 dBFS. Hero moment 1 needs the full keyed and effects treatment space is getting. See P1-P12.
2. **The doctor's head is cut off in most of her coverage** (15.3-18.2, 26.5-27.0, 180.5-183.8; partly 32-34). Every `cam_desk_top` shot frames her chest and hands. The `insert` tag on those cards turns the shot check's frame test off, so the gate never saw it. See C1.
3. **She "writes" with no pen in her hand and the pad out of reach.** `mx_writing_seated` plays over her lap while she sits sideways to the desk. The pen and clipboard lie on the desk. Her hands are clawed and spread in mid-air (16.5, 181.5). See A1.
4. **Captions are white with no outline on white walls.** About half the clinic's lines and the doctor's testimonial can't be read. See T1.
5. **No lab coat, no glasses, and a stethoscope that's two black hooks.** The doctor reads as a woman in a T-shirt. The script's "takes off her glasses" beat can't happen: the glasses are hidden for the whole film. See W1-W4.
6. **His planted symptom is missing.** The script plants "his fingers drum his knees, he hums", the setup for the scene-5 payoff. The build has a head-nod layer and no hum (3.0-6.0). See A6.
7. **Sets look like a test level.** The waiting-room wide has no chairs, no poster, no him and no props. The poster insert is on a blank white wall. The turkey is against a flat grey wall. The "outside" shot is a pale void with no building (64.8-66.6). The flight sky is a flat pale gradient over low-poly green triangles. See S1-S6.
8. **Missing comic beats from the script.** She never looks up for "In space." He never stands to go in. She never rolls her shoulders or takes off her glasses. There's no reaction from her after the punch. The ad's drink happens with no can in the drinking hand. See A2, A3, A8, A11, A15.
9. **The ad is staged in one tiny wide.** For tv_both and tv_thumbs (183.8-190.8) the hosts are about 15% of frame height. The giant can is cut off at frame right. Pink stool cushions pass through their hips. The product shot (`tv_can`) and the establishing wide (`tv_wide`) are written but not in the cut. See C10-C12, S7-S9.
10. **The ending is broken.** The safety card flashes the set between its pages (195.6-195.9). The end card dissolves into the set (200.7-202.8). The second punch's fist is a floating red ellipsoid with no arm (210.0). The film ends on 2.5 s of an empty pale set and 1.7 s of digital silence (220.9-223.5). The script's can roll (b_can) isn't in the cut. See T4, P13, E1.
