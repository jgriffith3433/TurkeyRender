## Tricks that worked

- **The point** is `Idle1PointOut` on `leftArm` with `pose` on `leftUpperArm`, because the arm follows the chest and each body needs its own correction: `[28,22,0]` on the snake, `[22,-8,0]` on house, `[26,10,0]` after the arm-up. Check it from the front.
- **Hand on hip over any legs:** `mx_catwalk_seq03` on `rightArm`, start 5.0, speed 0.4. The clip's body is turned, but the mask leaves that behind.
- **Back to the mark:** a `move` with `rel:1` during the spin, in world axes. A `keys` root rotates with the spin, so it swings him toward the lens and then pops.
- **Don't loop `mx_hh_booty_step` past 4.2:** the arm throws out. Slow it instead.

## Second pass (2026-11-07)

Judged baked, with `captures/dance/bake_look.mjs`, which rebakes and renders the take at its real place. `build_ch1.v1.mjs` is the first pass.

Clips read this time: `mx_jazz_square` (feet A, arms wide, so mask them with `mx_rib_pops`), `mx_jazz_arm_push` 0.55-2.2 at 1.3 (A, one arm up on each backbeat), `mx_house_3` 3.75-4.6 (A, a low wide drop) and 16.9-18.5 (B), `mx_northern_soul` 8.5-11 (B, side kicks; 1.6-7 is a slow turn away from the lens), `mx_slide_step` with a `move` on top (A: the bake turns the slide into real steps), `mx_pivot` (C, a boxing guard).

- **Backbeats are the odd beats** in all three takes. `pop()` in the build script is the accent: hip out, chest against it, head level, over 0.09 s with an overshoot. It survives the bake.
- **`speed` on a `layer` has no effect that I could see.** A layer over 4 beats wrapped the clip, and arms flew up. Split the layer in two instead.
- **Don't inertia-blend an arm down from overhead.** It made a 153 degree one-frame flip of the left shoulder at 127.03 s. `crossfade:1` cleared it.
