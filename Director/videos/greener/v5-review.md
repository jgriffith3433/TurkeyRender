# Greener v5: outside review

Video: `output/greener-v5.mp4`, 2:35, 1280x720, 30 fps, 4643 frames. Reviewed 2026-11-06. Times are video seconds; `f` numbers are frames (time x 30). Shots are named from `cards.map.json`. The picture usually cuts 1 frame after the card time.

Severity: A breaks the viewer out, B noticeable, C minor.

## Verdict

Not finished. The dance mostly holds up at 30 fps now: in the bulk of the chorus I found no elbow flips and no one-frame limb jumps. The bake did that work. What still looks broken is mostly elsewhere: the first frames of shots, the hand poses in close-up, a ceiling that flickers in every low-angle shot, and props that pop. The story's two big payoffs, taking the blade and "let go", don't read.

Worst single stretch: `hand_opens`, 139.4 to 142.8. It has the largest frame-difference spike in the whole video, and it's the end of the song.

## Coverage

- Metrics: all 4643 frames. Frame difference, a one-frame pop test and block-matching flow with camera motion taken out. Top 45 of each test, 119 frames, grouped into events, plus every whole-picture jump off a card cut. 42 events were pulled as 8-frame strips. Many of the rest are the ceiling flicker (ch_low, dv_low_2, c2_low_a, c2_low). Not every flagged frame got its own look, despite the brief: flow picks in din_wide and hall_empty, and some in alone_fog, c2_low and push, were judged by group only.
- Consecutive 30 fps frames: 79 of 116 40-frame sheets opened, about 3100 frames or two thirds of the video, including all of chorus 1, all of chorus 2 from c2_orbit_a on, and most of verse 2.
- Not read at 30 fps: the intro to 8.3 s, and gaps of 1-3 s at about 15-16, 19-20, 22-24, 28.5-29.5, 41-43, 44.5-45.8, 47-48.5, 78-80.5, 87-88, 95-96.5, 98-99, 100.5-102, 107-108.5, 110-111, 114-115, 116.5-118, 120.5-121.4, 136.5-139, 143-147, 148.5-150.5. Those had only the metrics and one frame per shot.
- Some sheets dropped out of my context after reading. Every A fault was re-read and confirmed from frames, except three: the second blade_up pop (metrics and an event strip I no longer have), and the flicker at ch_low_a, dv_low, c2_low_a and c2_low (metrics and my earlier notes; I re-confirmed the flicker itself in ch_low, dv_low_2, din_wide and c2_f5). Rows marked "notes" come from notes taken at the time and weren't re-read.
- Crops: sheets were cropped to him on medium and wide shots, and foot crops were cut at full resolution for 4 dance stretches. In wide shots he is under 300 px tall, so finger and ankle faults there can be missed.
- Not checked: audio, lip sync, and motion at real playback speed. These are frames read in order. J's eye at speed may catch what strips don't, and severity of a jump is my estimate of how it plays.
