# Greener: brief

J, 2026-11-06: make a new music video with the same character, sort of like Lily Allen's "The Fear". J's own breakdown of why that video works is the reference. Borrow its shape and irony, and make everything on screen our own. No shot-for-shot copying and none of its props or costume.

Song: "Gateway Drug", `Assets/Song/GatewayDrug.mp3`, 2:35 (J, 2026-11-06). Everything is measured in beats and sections, so `mv song` can reflow it.

## The idea

After touching grass, he decides he's fixed. Then the grass grows into a whole lifestyle. His scruffy backyard becomes a pastel estate of wellness and luxury, where everything is perfect and nothing is real. Satire of self-improvement as something to own. He sings all of it knowingly to camera, a deadpan face that is in on the joke, and he carries himself as if he is wearing a big party dress. He has no dress. Played straight, with total confidence.

## What we borrow

- Shabby start, fantasy, a grey dip, then the fantasy again by choice.
- Deadpan, knowing singing to camera against an absurd world.
- Playful, deliberately unsophisticated movement: how one would act in this type of music video.
- Symmetry, smooth tracking through rooms, cuts on the beat, things that come alive on hits.
- Pastel against grey.

## What is ours

- **Turkey butlers**, in bow ties, in perfect rows. They bow on the kick, hop in formation and carry trays. They are the dancers. Their history with him from TK Shock stays unspoken: he never looks at them straight, and a joke rests on that.
- **Grass as luxury:** one blade served on a silver tray under a cloche, a square of lawn in a gold frame, grass in a champagne glass.
- **Giant gift boxes** that hop on the beat, and balloons that bob in time.
- **The dip:** the estate is painted plywood flats held up with braces. Behind them is the real grey street, dead grass and a turkey butler alone on a break.
- **The ending:** the camera rises to show the estate tied in a huge ribbon under grey clouds, keeps pulling back, and it's a picture on the TV in his living room. He's on the sofa, knees together, deadpan, remote in hand. It's his TV fiasco all over again.

## The invisible dress (J, 2026-11-06)

"I want the character to act like they have a dress on and how one would act in this type of music video." This replaces the earlier "he never dances" and "waits for a bus".

He has no dress. He lifts an invisible skirt on stairs, swishes it, curtsies, walks with a hip-led glide, sits with knees together, flicks an imaginary hem, smooths it down, gives slow playful hand flourishes and sings knowingly to camera. Total confidence, played straight, never a wink at the joke of it. The face stays deadpan and knowing; the body does the party.

Performances: `dress_walk`, `dress_curtsy`, `dress_twirl`, `dress_lift_stairs`, `dress_sit`, tested in the gallery, from admitted clips and keys.

## Look

- **Fantasy:** bright polished light, pale pink, mint, baby blue, cream and gold. Clean, symmetrical, a bit too perfect. Bright artificial sky.
- **Dip:** the Touch Grass `soft-daylight` world with colour drained, flat overcast and long holds.
- **Camera:** mostly cuts and smooth tracking, no shake. Wide symmetrical frames against his close-ups to camera.

## Rules

J's standing rules apply: real-time 3D only, no AI video, everything on the beat, no Hawkes branding. Fear of bad dancing still counts: the movement is deliberately unsophisticated, never real choreography. No rented GPU for now, so sets and props come from packs, Blender or the engine.
