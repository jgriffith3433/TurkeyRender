# TK Shock storyboard

This replaces the shot lists. The video is an animation: **something must happen in every bar**. The camera shows events; it is not the event. Hold shots longer, cut when the story needs it, and move things, not lenses. Real-time 3D only, no AI video. Timing comes from `SONG.md`.

The story: a man watches TV. The TV watches back. It takes the room, then him, turns him into content, and moves on to the next viewer.

## Part 1: He watches (bars 1-13, sparse, then the vocal)

Set up everything that pays off later.

- **1-3.** Dark. Silhouette, one long push. TV is only snow. He's still, then his arm lifts the remote.
- **4-6.** Click. He channel surfs, one click on each strong note. Readable little shows flash by: a news desk, a weather map, a cooking show with a roast turkey, a game show. (These are the shows he'll be trapped in. Plant them clearly.)
- **7-8.** The vocal enters. He stops on a channel showing a dark room with a man on a sofa from behind. He doesn't get it yet.
- **9.** He scratches his neck. On TV the man does it a beat later. He freezes.
- **10.** He slowly raises a hand. The TV copies it. He turns to look up at the corner.
- **11.** The corner camera is already turning to meet him: its head rotates, red light brightens. Hold on that.
- **12.** Panic clicks. Nothing. He stabs the power button. The TV doesn't turn off, and brightens.
- **13.** He throws the remote or gets half up. The TV picture zooms in on him. He sinks back.

## Part 2: It wakes up (bars 14-24, drums, then they fall away)

- **14-16.** First full view of his face as he turns back to the TV, lit by it. TV flashes on the drums.
- **16-21.** The room starts to react, small and gradual: water shivers in a glass on the kick, a picture frame ticks crooked, curtains breathe toward the TV, a lamp flickers, dust drifts toward the screen. On TV a turkey silhouette walks through the snow and stops to look out. Each thing is a beat of its own.
- **22.** Music drops. Slow push to his eye.
- **23.** "Jolted": real eyelids snap wide, pupil blows open.
- **24.** He sees one TV become red, green and blue TVs. Things start to lean toward them.

## Part 3: The breakdown (bars 25-33)

The pull is **gradual**: it builds for all of Part 3 and is only total at the end.

- **25:1.** The light blast. **The couch falls backward with him on it**, landing at 25:3 in a slam of dust. Biggest single hit so far.
- **26.** Silence in the room after the slam, ringing light. He crawls out from behind the couch. Small things start to slide: the remote, the glass, papers.
- **27.** Carpet ripples and its corner lifts. Lamp drags, topples. Coffee table scrapes forward a little on each kick.
- **28.** The couch itself starts scraping toward the TV, with him holding on and being dragged.
- **29.** It stops. One turkey head pushes out through the glass, which stretches like skin. It climbs out, shakes, looks at him. A long, funny, scary held beat. Then a second one. A third.
- **30.** They come. He runs for the door.
- **31.** He gets the door open. Behind it: the same room, and himself at a door, seen from behind (or solid TV static). He turns. Turkeys.
- **32.** Flood of turkeys. Walls peel away one at a time toward the screen. He's swept off his feet.
- **33.** Music cuts: "Somebody's watching me". Everything hangs. The red light fills frame. On the TV: him, right now.

## Part 4: The fall (bars 34-40)

He drops through the floor of the world. The look changes on each phrase, as the treatment asks, but he's always doing something: tumbling, grabbing at turkeys, being pecked, riding one, hit by a falling sofa. Turkeys fall with him like hail. "I always feel like somebody's watching me" lands on the red eyes. The ground comes up: a white mountain.

## Part 5: Feathers (bars 41-51, the slow motion)

- **41:1.** He hits a mountain of feathers. Slow motion: a huge crown of feathers goes up. This is the slow-mo image.
- **42.** Feathers rain back down in slow motion. He surfaces, gasping. "I go to the eyes": his eye, close.
- **43-46.** He looks at his hand: **a feather has grown from his skin**. Then more, spreading up his arms, over his neck. He tries pulling them out. They keep coming. He's getting feathered.
- **47-48.** Overwhelmed, on hands and knees, he goes to throw up, and feathers pour out of his mouth.
- **49-51.** Instead of collapsing he's lifted, arms hanging out like wings, turning in the air. The camera swings around him, swept up. From high up he sees what the mountain is sitting on: it's pouring down into the TV.

## Part 6: Pulled in (bars 52-58)

The long, gradual suck-in. The biggest sequence in the video.

- **52-53.** The mountain drains like an hourglass into the TV, a giant now. He falls back into the funnel and is carried down with it, feathers stripped off him in streams.
- **54.** Red lights open in the dark, hundreds. "I always feel like somebody's watching me".
- **55.** Silence. He's caught the TV's edge with both hands. Frozen. Hanging on, body pulled out straight.
- **56.** Fingers slip, one at a time, on the beat.
- **57.** He goes. Feet first, his body stretches and thins into scan lines, limb by limb. His face is last, then one hand.
- **58.** Snow. Then a channel tuning in.

## Part 7: He's on TV (bars 59-68, the last drop)

Every set is a full 3D scene with action. He's in each show, more trapped each time, and each show ends by wrecking the next. Turkeys run the shows. Snow bursts and a channel number between them.

- **59. News.** He's the anchor, stiff. The turkey co-anchor in a red tie takes his script in its beak, then comes over the desk at him and flies out of the top of the frame.
- **60. Weather.** He points at a map as turkeys rain on it, then on him. The bird in the red tie lands on him. The whole map board falls on the frame.
- **61. Cooking.** Turkey chef bastes him on a platter. He crawls for the rim. The storm comes through the ceiling, the stove catches, the cloche lands on the last kick. The reversal is the joke.
- **62. Game show.** He spins strapped to the wheel while contestants slap buzzers, one in the chef's hat. The axle shears on the last kick and the wheel rolls off with him.
- **63. Workout.** Jumping jacks, sweatbands, 1-800 number. The wheel comes through the neon sun, breaks the class and misses him by a hand as he dives.
- **64. Sitcom.** His own sofa, a turkey audience. The class runs in through the door, the flats fall one by one, and behind them is a dark stage with a hundred red tally lights. The wheel crosses it.
- **65. Nature.** He's stalked in tall grass by a turkey with a camera, while the whole cast comes over the hill. The wheel is in the hillside. He runs.
- **66. Late night.** He's the guest, feathered, and starts to enjoy it. Something city-sized stands up behind the skyline. Nobody minds.
- **67-68.** Channels bleed: the news wall opens on the game show, the bare weather wall on the workout, the mob follows him through every set, every channel's graphics at once. He's stopped fighting, a host now. Biggest smile in the film. Pull back through a wall of TVs, every one of them him.

## Part 8: The next one (bars 69-73)

- **69-70.** Same room, a different person from the first frame: a cloud of curls, smaller, sideways on the sofa with her feet up. Bar 1's framing, then alongside her.
- **71.** Her thumb on the remote. Click. Click. Over her hair to the screen: she stops on him, feathered, smiling, and he waves at her.
- **72.** She stops moving. Up in the corner, a camera head turns.
- **73.** The last hit: the room goes, the red light comes on full and stays.

## Rules

- A shot exists to show an event. If nothing changes, cut the shot or add an event.
- Cause and effect: every reaction has a cause on screen.
- Fewer cuts than v6 in Parts 1, 2, 5 and 6. Fast cutting belongs to Parts 3, 4 and 7, and even there each cut shows something new.
- Characters act. Clips blend, they don't pop. Hands hold things. Things have weight.
- Comedy and dread together.

## The nemesis (added 2026-11-05)

One turkey runs the whole video: **the tie bird**. White, with a small red tie, always the same model and tie, and it always looks straight at him. It's the only bird that seems to think.

- **Bars 5-6:** a bird in a tie is visible on the news as he surfs past.
- **Bars 20-21:** the silhouette that stops and looks out of the TV wears the tie.
- **Bars 29-30:** the first bird through the glass has the tie.
- **Bars 34-40:** it's the bird he grabs, and the one that bites his fingers on "ME".
- **Bars 41-58:** it perches on the mountain, watching. At bar 56 it stands on the TV rim beside his fingers and takes the last slip personally.
- **Bars 59-68:** his co-anchor, and in some way present in every show.
- **Bars 69-73:** in the last show she stops on, it's at the desk. It looks out of the TV at her, then at the corner camera.

## The cameraman (J, 2026-11-05: "a camera man filming himself too, a triple fourth wall break")

**Bar 11-12.** He turns to the corner camera, and it turns to him. Cut behind it, in the dark, to the tie bird as cameraman: headphones on, wing on a big studio camera, a small monitor beside it.
1. The monitor shows him on the sofa looking into the lens (the room as the corner camera sees it).
2. Push in on the monitor: it now shows the tie bird from behind, filming, with the monitor inside it.
3. The bird slowly turns its head and looks straight at us, the real audience. Beat. Cut back to the room as the power clicks fail.

Three walls: he sees the camera, the camera films the cameraman, and the cameraman sees you.
Payoff in bar 72-73: the same stare before the last red dot.

## The realization, 1:54-2:07 (J, 2026-11-06)

J: this is an emotional moment where he sees the turkey and realizes the turkey is the enemy. It's not arms flailing.

Stillness, eyes, a slow cross-cut. He surfaces. The feathers settle. He looks around, lost. He sees the tie bird, perched, watching him, perfectly still. Close on him: it dawns. Close on the bird: it tilts its head. One feather lands on his hand. He looks at it, looks back at the bird, and he understands. Only then does anything happen, on the music.
