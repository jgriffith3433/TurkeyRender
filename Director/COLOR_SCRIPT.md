# Colour script and pace targets

One film, one arc of colour. The palette tells the story: the world starts as cold TV light in the dark, TV colour leaks into reality, reality goes, and everything ends as television.

## Colour by part

| Part | Bars | Palette | Light | Feeling |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 He watches | 1-13 | Near black, one cold blue-white, tiny red dot | TV only, deep shadow | Still, lonely |
| 2 It wakes up | 14-24 | Black, with TV colours starting to throw across the room | TV colour changes on the beat | Unease rising |
| 3 Breakdown | 25-33 | Hard white blast, then black with hot red emergency and cold TV blue | Two colours fighting, red for threat, blue for the TV | Violence |
| 4 The fall | 34-40 | Graphic: red and black, plus one new treatment per phrase | Flat, poster-like | Unreal |
| 5 Feathers | 41-51 | Pale cream feathers on deep blue-black, warm gold rising at the lift | Soft top light, a holy shaft at the lift | Beauty, quiet horror |
| 6 Pulled in | 52-58 | Deep red dark, hundreds of red points, white TV glare | The TV is the only big source | Dread, inevitability |
| 7 He's on TV | 59-68 | Saturated broadcast colours: each show has its own palette | Bright, flat studio light, the opposite of Part 1 | Manic, funny, wrong |
| 8 The next one | 69-73 | Exactly Part 1 | TV only, then one red dot | Cold, closed loop |

Rules:
- Red belongs to the watcher: the camera light, its glow, the eyes. Use it elsewhere only when it means being watched or danger.
- Cold blue-white is the TV. Warm colour is rare, so that the gold of the lift and the show sets feel like an event.
- Black stays truly black in Parts 1, 2 and 8. Part 7 is the only bright section, which makes it feel like a different world.
- Skin stays readable and lit by the scene's own source. Never flat grey.
- Part 8 must match Part 1 exactly, so the loop lands.

## Pace targets

Shot lengths in beats. One bar is 4 beats, about 2.76 seconds. These are starting targets from the song's energy: pick what the story needs, but explain a big miss.

| Part | Bars | Average shot | Longest shot allowed | Cuts per bar |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1-13 | 8-12 beats | 16 beats, with an event inside | 0.3-0.5 |
| 2 | 14-24 | 4-6 beats, shortening | 8 beats | 0.7-1 |
| 3 | 25-33 | 2-3 beats | 6 beats (the turkey stare) | 1.5-2 |
| 4 | 34-40 | 2-4 beats | 8 beats | 1-2 |
| 5 | 41-51 | 8-16 beats | 24 beats | 0.25-0.5 |
| 6 | 52-58 | 4-6 beats, with a very long hold at bar 55 | 8 beats | 0.7-1 |
| 7 | 59-68 | 2-4 beats within shows, flips at 1-2 bars, faster at 67-68 | 8 beats | 1-2, then 4 |
| 8 | 69-73 | 8+ beats | 16 beats | 0.3-0.5 |

Contrast is the point. The fast parts feel fast because the slow parts are truly slow. Cuts are not the only way to add energy: action inside the frame counts more. Never add a cut just to reach a number.
