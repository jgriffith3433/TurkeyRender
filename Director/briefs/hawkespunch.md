# Hawkes Punch: brief

J, 2026-11-07. Our character goes to the doctor. The doctor prescribes "Hawkes Punch" every day until the symptoms go away, then turns to him and says: "Don't worry, it's only just a healthy amount of people in the world, you are just 1 of them, so shut up and swallow the damn Hawkes Punch", and punches him. He is blown away into the stars, finds someone like him in space, and they play cat and mouse: chasing, scaring and hitting each other with ever bigger stunts, until they are roping whole worlds with lassos and throwing them at each other. Then they stop, get close, and talk about Hawkes Punch and whether it's right for you, and it becomes a commercial.

This is the third episode of the same man's life: the TV took him (TK Shock), the grass fixed him and then owned him (Greener), and now medicine. Hawkes Punch is a Hawkes Biomedical "product", so the name is on screen; the corporate logo and the "we own the category" line stay off, as J asked for the earlier videos.

## Look

- Clinic: clean, pale green and white, flat fluorescent, a little too bright; the poster is the only red.
- Space: deep blue-black, hard sunlight from one side, rim light, big saturated planets, stars that don't twinkle. Stunts read by silhouette against planets.
- Commercial: bright, pastel, over-lit, 2000s daytime TV.
- Camera: mostly cuts, calm, dead zone; long smooth tracks in space; no shake except the punch impact.
